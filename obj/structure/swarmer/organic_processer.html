organic_processer
/obj/structure/swarmer/organic_processer

Swarmer organic processer

Allows swarmers to process organic items, like fruits, vegetables, or reagents in containers. All items that can be processed are listed in code/modules/antagonists/swarmers/swarmer_acts/core_swarmer_acts.dm
