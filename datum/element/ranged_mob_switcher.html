ranged_mob_switcher
/datum/element/ranged_mob_switcher

Ranged attacks switcher for hostile mobs (not basic)

On right click on self opens up a radial menu containing specific ranged modes (/datum/ranged_mob_switcher_mode), passed in as a list.

Vars

mode_name_to_typeAssociative list in format: [mode_name] = mode_type
possible_modesList of all modes the mob can switch to, passed in Attach()
radial_menu_listAssociative list in format: [mode_name] = image()
switch_delayTime required to switch modes, passed in Attach()
