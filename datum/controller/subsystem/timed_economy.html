Schedules a subscription for future processing. Calculates the target bucket based on the subscription's interval and the current index.

MATH: target_bucket = ((current_index - 1 + ticks_to_wait) % BUCKET_COUNT) + 1 This formula ensures correct wrap-around behavior within the circular buffer.
