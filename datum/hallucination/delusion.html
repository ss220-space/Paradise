delusion
/datum/hallucination/delusion

A hallucination that makes us and (possibly) other people appear as something else.

Vars

affects_othersIf TRUE, this hallucination affects all humans in existence
affects_usIf TRUE, this delusion affects us
delusion_icon_fileThe file the delusion image is made from
delusion_icon_stateThe icon state of the delusion image
delusion_nameThe name of the delusion image
delusionsAn assoc list of affected mobs -> delusions of them we've made
durationThe duration of the delusions
play_wabbajackIf TRUE, we will play the wabbajack sound to the hallucinator
skip_nearbyIf TRUE, people in view of our hallucinator won't be affected (requires affects_others)
